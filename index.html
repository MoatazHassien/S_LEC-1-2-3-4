Data Science & Methodology

Lecture 1 — يعني إيه methodology، وإزاي بنحل أي مشكلة بالداتا

1.1 What is Data Science?

Definition
Data science is the domain of study that deals with vast volumes of data using modern tools and techniques to find unseen patterns, derive meaningful information, and make business decisions.

ببساطة: عندك داتا كتير جداً، والـ Data Science هو العلم اللي بيستخدم أدوات حديثة عشان يطلّع منها أنماط مش ظاهرة (unseen patterns) ومعلومات مفيدة تساعد الشركة تاخد قرار.

  • بيستخدم خوارزميات machine learning معقدة لبناء predictive models (نماذج بتتنبأ).
  • الداتا ممكن تيجي من مصادر كتيرة وبـ formats مختلفة (جداول، نصوص، صور، sensors…).
  • هو عملية كاملة: extraction → preparation → analysis → visualization → maintenance.
  • مجال cross-disciplinary (متعدد التخصصات): بياخد من الإحصاء والبرمجة والـ business، ويستخدم scientific methods عشان يطلع insights.

1.2 What is a Methodology?

Definition
Methodology is a general strategy that guides the processes and activities within a given domain.
Simple idea

الـ methodology زي وصفة الطبخ أو خريطة الطريق: مش بتقولك تفاصيل كل خطوة بالحرف، لكن بتقولك الخطوات الرئيسية وترتيبها عشان ماتتوهش.

1.3 Why does Data Science need a Methodology?

الناس اللي بتشتغل في الـ data science بتلاقي إجابات لأسئلة مختلفة كل يوم، فمحتاجين روتين ثابت يمشوا عليه. الـ data science methodology:

  • بتحدد الروتين (routine) لإيجاد حلول لمشكلة معينة.
  • هي عملية cyclic (دايرية — بترجع وتتكرر) بتوجّه الـ business analysts والـ data scientists.

والـ methodology بتجاوب على 3 مجموعات أسئلة:

1.5 Popular Frameworks

الفكرة العامة واحدة، بس كل framework بيركّز على حاجة. في الامتحان هيسألوك: كل framework بيتميز بإيه؟

CRISP-DMKDDIBM Data Science MethodologyData science lifecycle

① CRISP-DM (Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining)

One of the most detailed and well-known methodologies, covering the full cycle from business understanding to deployment.

② KDD (Knowledge Discovery in Databases)

A broader process than data mining, focusing on the higher-level goal of extracting useful knowledge from data.

A 10-step process that places a heavy emphasis on defining the problem and analytic approach before acquiring data.

④ Data Science Lifecycle

A generalized term for a process with 5 to 7 iterative steps, from business understanding to deployment, often used in academia and tutorials.

  2. Data collection
  3. Data cleaning and preparation
  4. Exploratory data analysis
  5. Model building and deployment

7-step version

  1. Business understanding
  2. Data mining (gather & scrape data)
  3. Data cleaning
  4. Data exploration
  5. Feature engineering
  6. Predictive modeling
  7. Data visualization

Analytic Understanding

Lecture 1 (الجزء التاني) — أول خطوتين في IBM methodology + الـ 4 أنواع

2.1 Step 1 — Business Understanding

Definition
Before solving any problem in the business domain, it needs to be understood properly. Business understanding forms a concrete base that leads to easy resolution of queries. We should have clarity of what the exact problem is.
  • السؤال الأساسي: What is the problem you are trying to solve?
  • لازم تطلب توضيح بدري (early clarification)، وإلا هتضيّع وقت وموارد في الاتجاه الغلط.
  • عشان تفهم السؤال لازم تفهم هدف الشخص اللي بيسأل (the goal of the person asking the question).
  • بعدها بنقسّم الأهداف (break down objectives) ونرتّبها حسب الأولوية (prioritize).
Simple idea

لو عميلك قال "عايز أعرف ليه المبيعات قلّت" — قبل أي كود لازم تسأل: قلّت إمتى؟ في أنهي منتج؟ وعايزها لإيه؟ ده كله Business Understanding. الخطوة دي بتحدد الداتا اللي هتستخدمها والـ approach اللي هتمشي عليه.

2.2 Step 2 — Analytic Understanding (Analytic Approach)

Definition
Analytic approach: How can you use data to answer the question? The second step is selecting the correct approach for the specific problem; it points back to the business understanding and helps identify what methods to use.
Note

الخطوة دي اتكتبت بالاسمين في المحاضرات: Analytic Understanding و Analytic Approach — نفس الحاجة. لو شفتهم في اختيارات MCQ مع بعض، ماتتلخبطش.

طب الـ Data Analytics نفسه يعني إيه؟

Data analytics refers to the techniques used to analyze data to enhance productivity and business gain. Data is extracted from various sources, cleaned and categorized to analyze behavioral patterns. The techniques and tools vary according to the organization or individual.

2.3 The 4 Types of Analytics

بعد ما تفهم المشكلة كويس، بتختار واحد من 4 approaches. فكّر فيهم كأنك بتسأل أربع أسئلة متدرجة:

1) Descriptive Analytics — hindsight

Contextualize data to generate information; answers questions about occurred events.
  • What was the sales volume over the past 12 months?
  • What is the monthly commission earned by each sales agent?
  • التقارير static وبتعرض historical data في data grids or charts.
  • الـ queries بتتنفذ على operational data stores زي OLTP / CRM / ERP ← النتيجة reports & dashboards.

2) Diagnostic Analytics — insight

Determine the cause of a phenomenon that occurred in the past (the reason behind the event). AKA statistical analysis.
  • Why were Q2 sales less than Q1 sales?
  • Why was there an increase in patient re-admission rates over the past three months?
  • محتاج داتا من مصادر متعددة مخزنة بشكل يسمح بـ drill-down و roll-up (OLAP).
  • بتتشاف عن طريق interactive visualization tools عشان تلاقي trends و patterns.

3) Predictive Analytics — foresight

Predicts outcomes of events based on patterns, trends and exceptions from historical and current data.
  • What are the chances a customer will default on a loan if they missed a monthly payment?
  • If a customer bought products A and B, what are the chances they will also buy product C?
  • بيستخدم لـ identify risks and opportunities.
  • محتاج skillset أكبر من descriptive و diagnostic، ومحتاج large datasets.
  • النماذج معتمدة على ظروف الماضي ← لازم تتحدّث (update is required).

4) Prescriptive Analytics — foresight

Builds upon predictive results by describing actions that should be taken. An advisory approach: propose the best action and the reason.
  • Among three drugs, which one provides the best results?
  • When is the best time to trade a particular stock?
  • بيدّي أعلى قيمة (most valuable outcome) ومحتاج أصعب skillset.
TypeKey questionTime viewLevel
DescriptiveWhat happened?HindsightLow complexity
DiagnosticWhy did it happen?InsightMedium
PredictiveWhat will happen? (forecasting)ForesightHigh
PrescriptiveHow do we solve it? / What should we do?ForesightHighest — most advanced skillset
MCQ Trap

الحيلة: بص على أول كلمة في السؤال — "What / how many / what was…" ← Descriptive · "Why…" ← Diagnostic · "What will happen / chance that / forecast" ← Predictive · "Which is best / when is the best time / what should we do" ← Prescriptive.

وكمان: الـ Diagnostic اسمه التاني statistical analysis، والـ Predictive اسمه forecasting.

2.4 Question types → which model?

نوع السؤال اللي عايز تجاوب عليه هو اللي بيحدد نوع الـ model:

3.1 Data & Datasets

Data
Data is anything that has been produced or created during research. Primary data is data you created yourself; your datasets can also contain data created by other researchers.
Dataset
A collection of related data that share the same set of attributes or properties. Each member of the dataset is called a "datum".
MCQ Trap

عنصر واحد في الـ dataset اسمه datum (مفرد)، والجمع data. ومعظم الأسئلة بتسأل: "each member of a dataset is called…؟"

3.5 Data Objects & Attributes

Definition
Datasets are made up of data objects. A data object represents an entity (e.g., customers, store items, sales / patients, treatments / students, professors, courses). Also called samples, examples, instances, data points, objects, tuples. Data objects are described by attributes. Database rows → data objects; columns → attributes.
Definition
Attribute (or dimension, feature, variable): a data field representing a characteristic or feature of a data object. E.g., Customer_ID, name, address.

3.6 Attribute Types

(أ) Qualitative — الصفات اللي مش أرقام بالمعنى الحسابي

Nominal

Categories, states, or "names of things" — مفيش ترتيب.

Hair_color = {auburn, black, blond, brown, grey, red, white}; marital status, occupation, ID numbers, zip codes

Binary

Nominal attribute بـ حالتين بس (0 و 1).

Symmetric: الحالتين مهمين بنفس القدر (مثال: Gender).

Asymmetric: مش بنفس الأهمية (مثال: medical test positive vs negative). Convention: نديّ الـ 1 للنتيجة الأهم (مثلاً HIV positive).

Ordinal

القيم لها ترتيب معنوي (ranking) بس الفرق بين قيمتين متتاليتين مش معروف.

Size = {small, medium, large}, grades, army rankings

(ب) Quantitative (Numeric) — أرقام فعلاً

Interval-scaled

  • بتتقاس على مقياس وحداته متساوية (equal-sized units) والقيم ليها ترتيب.
  • No true zero-point (الصفر مش معناه "مفيش حاجة").
  • أمثلة: Temperature in °C or °F, calendar dates.

Ratio-scaled

  • ليه inherent zero-point (صفر حقيقي).
  • تقدر تقول إن قيمة "ضعف" قيمة تانية: 10 K is twice as high as 5 K.
  • أمثلة: Temperature in Kelvin, length, counts, monetary quantities.
Simple idea

ليه 20°C مش "ضعف" 10°C؟ لأن الصفر في Celsius اتحدد اعتباطي (تجمّد المية). لكن في Kelvin الصفر هو "انعدام الحرارة" فعلاً، فـ 10K ضعف 5K. وفلوسك: 0 جنيه يعني معاكش فلوس، و200 ضعف 100 ← Ratio.

(ج) Discrete vs Continuous

Discrete attributeContinuous attribute
ValuesOnly a finite or countably infinite set of valuesHas real numbers as values
ExamplesZip codes, profession, set of words in a collection of documentsTemperature, height, weight
RepresentationSometimes integer variables; binary attributes are a special case of discreteTypically floating-point variables (practically measured with a finite number of digits)

  • Celsius/Fahrenheit/dates = Interval · Kelvin/length/counts/money = Ratio.
  • Grades / army rank / small-medium-large = Ordinal (ترتيب بدون مسافات معروفة).
  • Gender = Symmetric binary · Test positive/negative = Asymmetric binary.
  • الـ Binary هو Nominal بحالتين بس.

Lecture 2 (Part 1) — خطوات 3 و4 و5 من IBM methodology

4.1 Step 3 — Data Requirements

Definition
The chosen analytical method indicates the necessary data content, formats and sources to be gathered. During this process, find the answers to what, where, when, why, how & who. Here you define the data you need to solve the business problem — the format of data and what particular data should be collected.
Example

داتا ساينتست في بنك عايز يتنبأ بأهلية القرض (predict loan eligibility) ← محتاج: monthly income, profession, age, years in the country.

Simple idea

الـ Data Requirements هي "قايمة المشتريات": قبل ما تنزل السوق (تجمع الداتا) لازم تعرف إنت محتاج إيه بالظبط. والـ approach اللي اخترته في الخطوة 2 هو اللي بيحدد القايمة.

4.2 Step 4 — Data Collection

Definition
Data collection is the process of collecting, measuring and analyzing different types of information using a set of standard validated techniques. The main objective is to gather information-rich and reliable data and analyze it to make critical business decisions.
  • بتيجي بعد ما تظبط الـ requirements. ممكن تكون live streamed data من streaming analytics services، أو الشركة تدّيك الـ dataset، أو تستخدم web scraping (للـ textual/digital data).
  • معظم الداتا في الخطوة دي raw ← بعدها لازم cleaning و processing عشان تبقى مفيدة.

Primary data collection

Data collected first-hand directly from the main source; data that has never been used in the past; generally regarded as the best kind of data in research.

تنقسم إلى: quantitative methods (بتتعامل مع حاجات تتعدّ) و qualitative methods (مش لازم تكون أرقام).

Secondary data collection

Data that has already been collected by someone else; much more inexpensive and easier to collect than primary data.

Primary methods — بالتفصيل

MethodDetails (exam wording)
1. InterviewsA direct method: the interviewer asks, the interviewee responds; high flexibility (questions can be adjusted anytime).
• Structured: specific set of questions, same order to all respondents → allows comparison between participants.
• Unstructured: no fixed set; free-flowing conversation; the interviewer controls/steers the discussion based on answers.
• Semi-structured: combines both — pre-determined questions + additional questions based on answers.
2. ObservationsResearchers observe a situation and record findings; controlled (everyone knows they're observed) vs uncontrolled (no one knows).
• Simple (non-participant): observe from a distance without participating (avoid influencing behavior).
• Participant: researcher participates in the activity; deeper understanding of behaviors and relationships.
• Structured: pre-established plan/methodology (decide in advance what & how to record).
• Unstructured: flexible and spontaneous, no pre-set plan; record everything that might be relevant.
3. Surveys & QuestionnairesBroad perspective from large groups; face-to-face, mailed, or posted on the internet to reach respondents anywhere.
4. Focus groupsSimilar to an interview but with a group who share something in common; gives better understanding of why a certain group thinks in a particular way.
5. Oral historiesLike interviews/focus groups but more precisely defined — linked to a single phenomenon; collects opinions and personal experiences of people involved in a particular event.

الفرق بين الـ 3 interviews: structured = نفس الأسئلة بنفس الترتيب · unstructured = كلام حر · semi-structured = الاتنين. وفي observation: simple (non-participant) = بتتفرج من بعيد · participant = داخل في النشاط. وfocus group مجموعة عندها حاجة مشتركة، أما oral history مرتبطة بحدث واحد.

4.3 Data Scraping & Web Scraping

Definition
Data scraping is a technology that extracts data — in large quantities and well-organized formats — from websites in an automated manner. Extracted data supports business analysis to draw insights and make marketing decisions.

Web scraping is an automated technique to extract data from websites instead of manual copy-paste (slow & repetitive). Tools can be custom-built or used across multiple sites. Many websites restrict saving data, so scraping offers a way to access information within legal limits.

Uses of web scraping

Market & competitor analysisFinancial data collectionSocial media monitoringSEO trackingResearch & machine learning
  • Market & Competitor Analysis: scrape product prices, customer reviews, competitor offerings.
  • Financial Data Collection: real-time stock prices, historical data, financial reports.
  • Social Media Monitoring: track trending topics, customer sentiment, campaign effectiveness.
  • SEO Tracking: monitor how websites rank on search engines for keywords over time.
  • Research & Machine Learning: collect large datasets to train ML models.

الخلاصة: scraping بيخلّي جمع الداتا أسرع، scalable، وأدق من الطرق اليدوية.

4.4 Step 5 — Data Understanding

Definition
Data understanding answers the question: "is the data collected representative of the problem to be solved?" Descriptive statistics calculates measures applied over data to assess the content and quality. This step may lead to reverting back to the previous step for correction.
  • أول سؤال تسأله لنفسك: "is this data going to answer the problem that I am having?" وبعدها تبدأ Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
  • في الـ EDA بتعمل visualizations و graphs عشان تلاقي العلاقات بين الأعمدة (مثال: المرتب مقابل سنين الخبرة).
  • الخطوة دي ممكن ترجّعك لـ Data Collection (السهم الدوبل في الـ diagram) لو الداتا ناقصة أو مش مناسبة.

Data Exploration & Basic Statistics

Lecture 2 (Part 2) — mean / median / mode والـ skewness (فيها حسابات!)

5.1 Data Exploration

Definition
A preliminary exploration of the data to better understand its characteristics. Key motivations: helping to select the right tool for preprocessing or analysis, and making use of humans' abilities to recognize patterns (people can recognize patterns not captured by data analysis tools). Related to Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

5.2 Basic Statistical Descriptions of Data

  • Motivation: نفهم الداتا أحسن: central tendency, variation and spread.
  • Data dispersion characteristics: median, max, min, quantiles, outliers, variance, etc.
  • الـ numerical dimensions بتتقابل مع sorted intervals، والتحليل بيتعمل بـ boxplot أو quantile analysis.
Simple idea

عشان توصف ناس في فصل بسرعة: الـ center (متوسط الأعمار كام؟) = central tendency — والانتشار (فيه واحد عمره 10 وواحد 70؟) = dispersion.

5.3 Measuring the Central Tendency

① Mean (algebraic measure)

Sample mean x̄ = 1n Σi=1n xi    |    Population mean μ = ΣxN

n = حجم الـ sample · N = حجم الـ population.

Weighted arithmetic mean

A type of average used when some values contribute more to the final result than others — each value has a weight representing its importance.
Weighted mean x̄w = Σ wi xiΣ wi
Example

Homework 80 (w=1) · Project 90 (w=3) · Final 70 (w=2):

x̄w = (1×80) + (3×90) + (2×70)1 + 3 + 2 = 4906 = 81.67

Trimmed mean

Trimmed mean: chopping (removing) the extreme values from both ends, then averaging the rest.
Example

Data: 10, 12, 13, 15, 16, 100. نشيل 1 value من كل طرف (10 و 100) ← باقي 12, 13, 15, 16:

x̄t = 12+13+15+164 = 14   vs   ordinary mean = 1666 ≈ 27.7

يعني الـ trimming شال تأثير القيمة الشاذة (100).

② Median

Median: the middle value if the number of values is odd, or the average of the two middle values otherwise (after sorting). For grouped data it is estimated by interpolation.
Odd

data: 4, 7, 9, 10, 12 → median = 3rd value = 9

Even

data: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 → (7 + 9)/2 = 8

Simple idea

تخيل مرتبات شركة: قلة بياخدوا ملايين ← الذيل ناحية اليمين (positive skew) ← الـ mean بيتشدّ لفوق وبيبقى أكبر من الـ median. عشان كده في المرتبات بنفضّل الـ median — لأنه robust مش بيتأثر بالـ outliers.

MCQ Trap

الـ mean هو الأكثر تأثراً بالـ outliers، والـ median الأقل، والـ trimmed mean علاج لمشكلة الـ outliers. والـ weighted mean بيتحسب بقسمة Σwx على Σw (مش على n).

Similarity & Dissimilarity

Lecture 3 — أكتر chapter فيه مسائل وقوانين. ركّز فيه!

6.1 Basics

Similarity

Numerical measure of how alike two objects are. Value is higher when objects are more alike. Often in range [0, 1].

Dissimilarity

Measure of how different two objects are. Lower when more alike. Minimum = 0; the upper limit varies.

Proximity

Refers to either a similarity or a dissimilarity.

For [0,1] scaling:   sim = 1 − d   and   d = 1 − sim

Matrices

Data matrix

  • N objects × p attributes
  • Two-mode (rows ≠ columns)

Dissimilarity matrix

  • N × N, triangular (symmetric)
  • Single-mode
  • Diagonal = 0 (object with itself)
  • Used in clustering & classification

Properties of a metric (distance)
  1. Positive definiteness: d(x,y) ≥ 0, and d(x,y) = 0 only if x = y
  2. Symmetry: d(x,y) = d(y,x)

6.3 Nominal attributes

Simple Matching SMC = mp   |   d(i,j) = p − mp = 1 − SMC

m = عدد الـ attributes المتطابقة (matches) · p = إجمالي عدد الـ attributes.

Example

p = 4 attributes، اتطابق منهم m = 2 ← d = (4−2)/4 = 0.5، و SMC = 0.5.

One-hot (binary encoding) method

نحوّل كل nominal attribute لـ binary columns (واحدة لكل قيمة)، ونحسب عدد الـ bits المختلفة / إجمالي الـ bits.

Example (tennis D1 vs D2)

بعد الـ one-hot طلع 10 bits واختلفوا في 4 bits ← d = 4/10 = 0.4 (بينما الـ simple mismatch على الـ original attributes = 0.5). يعني الطريقتين ممكن يدّوا أرقام مختلفة — اقرأ السؤال يقول إيه.

6.4 Binary attributes

Contingency table (objects i and j)

j = 1j = 0
i = 1qr
i = 0st

q = both 1 · t = both 0 · r, s = mismatch · p = q + r + s + t

Symmetric binary

الـ 0 و 1 نفس الأهمية (مثال: gender).

SMC = q + tq+r+s+t
d = r + sq+r+s+t

Asymmetric binary

حالة واحدة نادرة/مهمة (مثال: test positive). t بتتتجاهل. الـ 1 = الأهم.

Jaccard = qq+r+s
d = r + sq+r+s
MCQ Trap

Symmetric ← الـ denominator فيه t (q+r+s+t). Asymmetric ← من غير t (q+r+s). والـ Jaccard = q/(q+r+s) دي similarity، والـ d = 1 − Jaccard.

Example (Jack, Mary, Jim — asymmetric)
NameFeverCoughTest-1Test-2Test-3Test-4
JackYNPNNN
MaryYNPNPN
JimYPNNNN

Y,P = 1 · N = 0.

d(Jack,Mary) = 0+12+0+1 = 0.33    d(Jack,Jim) = 1+11+1+1 = 0.67    d(Jim,Mary) = 1+21+1+2 = 0.75

أقرب اتنين = Jack و Mary (أقل dissimilarity).

6.5 Standardization: z-score & MAD

لو الـ attributes بمقاييس مختلفة (income بالآلاف و age بالعشرات)، الـ distance هتتسيطر عليها الأكبر. الحل: نعمل standardization قبل ما نحسب distance عشان كل المتغيرات تساهم بالتساوي.

z-score z = x − μσ   ⟹   mean = 0, SD = 1
Example

x = 70, μ = 60, σ = 5 ← z = (70−60)/5 = 2 (يعني أعلى من المتوسط بـ 2 SD).

مثال Income/Age: income SD = 32,660 (population) و age SD = 15 (sample) ← بعد الـ z-score القيم بقت ±1.22 و ±1.00 ← دلوقتي الاتنين بنفس الـ scale.

Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD) — more robust than SD sf = 1n Σ |xif − mf|   ,   zif = xif − mfsf
Example

Data [3, 5, 7] ← m = 5 ← s = (|3−5|+|5−5|+|7−5|)/3 = 4/3 = 1.33.

Note

الـ MAD بيستخدم absolute deviation بدل الـ squared ← الـ outliers تأثيرها أقل ← more robust.

6.6 Ordinal attributes

  1. استبدل كل قيمة بـ rank r (1 … M).
  2. حوّلها لـ [0,1]: z = r − 1M − 1
  3. عامِلها كـ interval-scaled (استخدم Manhattan/Euclid).
Example

Education: High school < Bachelor < Master < PhD ← M = 4 ← 0, 0.33, 0.67, 1.0.

6.7 Mixed-type attributes

Mixed dissimilarity d(i,j) = Σf δij(f) · dij(f)Σf δij(f)
  • δ = 1 لو القياس موجود (مفيش missing)، و δ = 0 لو الـ value ناقصة أو (للـ asymmetric binary) الاتنين 0.
  • كل attribute بيتحسب بالـ rule بتاعه: nominal/binary → 0 لو متطابق و 1 لو مختلف · numeric → |x−y|/(max−min) · ordinal → rank normalization.
Example

لو 2 attributes، δ = 1 لكل واحد، و d(1) = 0.4 و d(2) = 0.3 ← d = 0.7 / 2 = 0.35.

Heads-up — slide inconsistency

في مثال الـ A/B/C بتاع الـ Euclidean، الأرقام اللي على الـ slide (18.7 / 21.8 / 38.7) مش بتطابق القانون — لما نطبق الـ formula بالظبط بيطلع A–B ≈ 21.21، A–C ≈ 26.93، B–C ≈ 43.87. في الامتحان طبّق القانون، وماتعتمدش على الرقم الأصلي لو الاختيارات قريبة منه.

Cheat Sheet 1 — Concepts & Rules

Data Science Methodology

Frameworks

  • CRISP-DM: 6 phases (Business Und., Data Und., Data Prep., Modeling, Evaluation, Deployment)
  • KDD: 5 steps (Selection, Preprocessing, Transformation, Data Mining, Interpretation)
  • IBM (Rollins): 10 steps
  • Generic cycle: 7 steps (iterative)

4 Analytics types

TypeQuestion
DescriptiveWhat happened?
DiagnosticWhy did it happen?
PredictiveWhat will happen?
PrescriptiveWhat should we do?

Value & complexity ↑ : Desc → Diag → Pred → Presc

Trick: read the first word of the question.

Attribute types

  • Nominal: names, no order (zip code, ID, color)
  • Binary: 2 states; symmetric / asymmetric
  • Ordinal: ordered, unknown gaps
  • Interval: equal gaps, no true zero (°C)
  • Ratio: true zero (height, weight, Kelvin)
  • Discrete = countable · Continuous = real numbers

Data forms

  • Structured: tables / DB
  • Semi-structured: JSON, XML
  • Unstructured: text, images, video

Collection

  • Primary: interviews, observation, surveys, focus groups
  • Secondary: existing reports, databases, published data
  • Scraping tools: Beautiful Soup, Requests, Scrapy, Selenium, Playwright

Skewness

  • Positive (right tail): Mode < Median < Mean
  • Negative (left tail): Mean < Median < Mode
  • Symmetric: Mean = Median = Mode
  • Mean most affected by outliers; median robust
  • Unimodal / bimodal / trimodal = 1 / 2 / 3 modes

Similarity facts

  • Similarity ∈ [0,1], higher = more alike
  • Dissimilarity min = 0; sim = 1 − d
  • Proximity = sim or dissim
  • Data matrix N×p (two-mode); dissimilarity matrix N×N (single-mode, diagonal 0, triangular)
  • Metric: positive definiteness, symmetry, triangle inequality

Which measure?

  • Numeric → Minkowski (z-score first)
  • Nominal → simple matching / one-hot
  • Symmetric binary → SMC (uses t)
  • Asymmetric binary → Jaccard (ignores t)
  • Ordinal → rank → z=(r−1)/(M−1)
  • Mixed → Σδd / Σδ

Exam traps

  • Analytic Understanding = Analytic Approach
  • Zip code / ID = nominal (not numeric)
  • Asymmetric → t is ignored
  • Grouped median: search N/2, subtract CF before, multiply by width
  • Weighted mean divides by Σw
  • L∞ ≤ L2 ≤ L1
